My fifth week in the AIDA lab explored model refinement, natural language processing, and workshops bridging machine learning with hearing science. Here are the highlights from my fifth week:

- **Optimizing Advanced CNN Architecture:** Improved on building a VGGNet for image classification on the CIFAR-10 dataset and successfully implemented a ResNet architecture from scratch as well, exploring how residual blocks and skip connections help train deeper neural networks more effectively.
- **Exploring Sentiment Analysis:** Moving from computer vision tasks to natural language processing, I implemented and compared performance across multiple architectures, including standard CNNs, Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs) using Long-Short Term Memory (LSTM) and Bidirectional LSTM, and hybrid approaches to perform sentiment classification on an IMDB dataset.
- **Computational Audiology Pre-Conference Workshops:** Attended "A Practical Introduction to Machine Learning for Hearing Science" by Dr. Mark Saddler and "Computational Models of the Auditory Periphery: From Normal Hearing to Impairment" by Ms. Annesya Banerjee, which provided hands-on exposure to machine learning concepts in hearing research and strategies for simulating auditory processing. These workshops were an excellent intersection between my technical studies and clinical interests.

From computer vision to natural language processing and real-world applications in hearing science, this week has delved into a wide variety of topics. Thanks for being here!
