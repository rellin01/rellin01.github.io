My seventh week in the AIDA lab was about refining multi-modal search performance for information retrieval, learning complex evaluation metrics, and beginning a new mentorship connection. Here are the highlights from my seventh week:

- **Industry Mentorship Introduction**: Met with industry mentor, Sindhuja, for an introductory sync to outline goals for the summer project and discuss where I am at in my learning process.
- **Semantic Search Engine Project Progress Meeting**: Reviewed current findings on semantic image search engine project with Shuo. We discussed my implementation built in PyTorch using Open AI's CLIP and Google Gemini to search the CIFAR-100 dataset.
- **Learning Data Analysis & Metrics**: Worked extensively on the evaluation of the search engine's performance. I explored dataset analytics through t-SNE visualization and comparison of one-shot vs logistic regression classification, retrieval metrics through cosine similarity, and performance metrics, including Precision@k, Recall@k, Mean Average Precision@k (mAP@k), Mean Reciprocal Rank (MRR), and Normalized Discounted Cumulative Gain (NDCG).

Thanks for following along. Looking forward to sharing what comes next!
