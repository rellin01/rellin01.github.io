My tenth week in the AIDA lab involved synthesizing my ongoing literature review, refining my organization strategy, and working on initial setup for the next phase of the fellowship experience. 
Here are the highlights from my tenth week:

- **Mentorship & Progress Review**: I met with Sindhuja to discuss strategies for organizing and tracking trends observed as I complete my literature review, such as using structured spreadsheet methods to highlight key topics across papers. We also discussed a broad synthesis of what I've learned from the literature review so far.
- **Continuing the Literature Review**: My readings this week went further into audio-language modeling and retrieval, focusing on several overarching themes:
  - Fine-Grained Temporal Grounding: Exploring methods to bridge temporal gaps in large audio-language models and enhance precise moment localization.
  - Models Handling Long-Audio: Looking at frameworks for frame-wise language-audio modeling and working with long-form audio datasets, such as CASTELLA, that have captions and temporal boundaries.
  - Retrieval-Augmented Generation: Looking at audio-integrated retrieval-augmented generation for spoken dialogue models which were inspired by video moment detection
  - Multi-Modal Foundations: Reviewing foundational work on contrastive learning for general-purpose audio representations and parts of an academic dissertation on content-based audio retrieval.
- **AIDA Lab Team Meeting**: I met with Shuo and Sindhuja to outline next steps for characterizing baseline data as we move towards implementation.
- **Infrastructure Troubleshooting**: I began working on setting up the computational environment, hitting some roadblocks while troubleshooting equipment configuration to SSH into the Tufts HPC, as well as encountering dependency incompatibility issues while trying to test using Google Colab.

Thanks for following along as I navigate both the theoretical and technical sides of the project. Looking forward to sharing updates next week!
