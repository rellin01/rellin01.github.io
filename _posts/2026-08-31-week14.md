My fifteenth week in the AIDA lab focused on reviewing more of the top-performing DCASE 2026 Task 6 technical reports, working to resolve and run Clotho-Moment pretraining, and refining our project's research direction. Here are the highlights:

- **Literature Review & Architecture Analysis**: Analyzed key technical reports from DCASE 2026 Task 6 challenge, including top-performing models using QAM-DETR and QD-DETR architectures. Focused on trying to understand their underlying model architecture, how temporal information is captured, and unique structural modifications compared to the baseline model.
- **Pre-training & Code Exploration**: Worked through troubleshooting and executing the Clotho-Moment pre-training pipeline and continued to explore the codebase to better understand the base model architecture and loss metrics.
- **HPC & Slurm Troubleshooting**: Learning how to configure and execute batch jobs on Tufts HPC Slurm scheduler.
- **Mentorship & Lab Collaboration**: Met with industry mentor Sindhuja to walk through the codebase and analyze TensorBoard outputs. We also refined the research question based on observed challenge trends that top-performing models consistently altered text and audio encoders while keeping the DETR architecture intact, suggesting feature embeddings are a key factor in performance.

Thanks for reading! Looking forward to sharing more next week.
