My sixth week in the AIDA lab focused on continuing hands-on implementation of deep learning architectures for NLP and computer vision tasks and exploring the fascinating intersection of machine learning and hearing science. Here are the highlights from my sixth week:

- **Computer Vision and NLP Code Review:** Conducted a code review with Shuo focused on the past two projects. This included evaluating MLP and CNN to optimize model performance on image classification tasks for the Fashion-MNIST dataset and comparing CNNs, RNNs using LSTMs and Bidirectional LSTMs, and hybrid approaches to perform sentiment analysis on IMDB movies review dataset.
- **Semantic Search:** Initiated my next major project to build a semantic image search engine using the CIFAR-100 dataset. This workflow leverages multi-modal models integrating OpenAI's CLIP and Google Gemini.
- **Lab Sync & Project Strategy:** Met with Shuo to discuss current project progress and map out upcoming milestones. Moving forward, I will begin collaborating with an industry mentor.
- **High-Performance Computing (HPC) Onboarding:** Met with AIDA lab member, Ishmam, for a tutorial on the Tufts HPC cluster. We also discussed other resource allocation strategies for heavy GPU workloads, such as cloud-based cluster solutions like RunPod.
- **Bridging Tech and Hearing Science:** Attended the Virtual Conference in Computational Audiology 2026. These sessions offered inspiring insights into current research across disciplines ranging from machine learning and AI for auditory and vestibular research to clinical implications for hearing aids and telehealth. I also shared these resources during our sync meeting to foster further collaborative exploration.

From scaling up architectures to mapping out multimodal search engines and diving into current applications, this week has been a massive step forward. Thanks for being here!
