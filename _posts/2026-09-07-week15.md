My sixteenth week in the AIDA lab focused on establishing the research pipeline to explore my research question, navigating dataset challenges, creating and resolving bugs in feature extraction scripts, and working on the final report. Here are the highlights:

- **Research Pipeline and Final Paper Set up**: Began drafting the final research paper and finalized the computational pipeline to address my research question regarding the influence of audio and text feature embeddings.
- **Dataset Acquisition and Preprocessing**: Worked on downloading the original Clotho-Moment and Castella datasets. Partnered with undergraduate labmate colleague Tom to troubleshoot YouTube download timeouts for the Castella audio datasets with resolution identified through cookie management to bypass rate limits, and also handled updating train, validation, and test paths due to audio files that were missing from being no longer listed on YouTube. Also identified and managed for discrepancies between dataset structure and the baseline code's train, validation, and test JSON formats.
- **Feature Extraction and Debugging**: Developed feature extraction scripts for OpenFLAM, LAION-CLAP, and M2D-CLAP. While starting to run feature extractions using M2D-clap, I discovered and resolved a bug in initial script logic where initial interpretation of the model's README documentation led to unintentional global average pooling step that flattened the temporal sequences. Corrected the pipeline to preserve sliding-window temporal structure and re-ran feature extractions for M2D-CLAP and began running feature extractions for OpenFLAM, while Tom focused on LAION-CLAP. Also started training the model to explore how these new embeddings change intersection over the union metrics.
- **Mentorship and Team Collaboration**: met with industry mentor Sindhuja to discuss how to adapt, integrate, and deploy feature-extraction third-party models within a research pipeline.Joined the weekly lab meeting to share progress, align on team goals, and discuss current model training workflows.
