My ninth week in the AIDA lab marked an exciting pivot point. With the multi-modal AI deep learning projects wrapped up, my focus shifted heavily toward literature reviews, team strategy sessions, and refining my approach to navigating complex deep learning research as we proceed to the next phases of my fellowship project.
Here are the highlights from my ninth week:
- **Industry Mentorship & Research Review**: I met with my industry mentor, Sindhuja, for a discussion on how to effectively analyze audio deep learning research articles. We focused on approaches to reading papers and how to extract information regarding current State-of-the-Art (SOTA) benchmarks, dataset design, and novel model architectures.
- **AIDA Lab Team Meeting**: We had a team meeting to discuss the current landscape of Audio Moment Retrieval (AMR) research and spent time identifying gaps and unanswered questions, technical needs, and the overall direction of recent work in this developing area of the computer audition field.
- **Beginning the Literature Review**: I officially started my literature review to ground my upcoming work. My readings this week followed the evolution of language-based audio retrieval, covering:
   - Early Foundations: Initial research exploring audio retrieval using natural language queries.
   - Contrastive Learning: Seminal papers on the CLAP (Contrastive Language-Audio Pre-training) model.
   - Current SOTA: The development of the AMR task, novel datasets to address unanswered questions like the Clotho-Moment and specialized model architectures like Audio Moment DETR.
   - Current Challenges: Investigating structural limitations of current audio-text and text-audio models with preserving sequential and concurrent temporal ordering in complex natural language queries (e.g. distinguishing between "a dog barking then the doorbell rang" and "a dog barking as the doorbell rang").

Thanks for following along as I move into literature review. Looking forward to sharing how these concepts shape the project next week!
