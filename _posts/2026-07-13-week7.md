My eighth week in the AIDA lab focused on wrapping up my semantic search engine project, exploring audio signal processing for speech recognition, and the opportunity to engage in valuable mentorship discussions. Here are highlights from my eighth week:

- **Semantic Search Engine Finalization**: Completed the analysis write-up and conducted a code clean-up for the multi-modal semantic image search engine project.
- **Audio Classification & Keyword Command Recognition**: Completed a project focused on speech recognition using short audio clips from the Speech Commands dataset. I implemented and evaluated two CNN architectures, one model trained on plain spectrograms and the other trained on Mel-spectrograms (experimenting with 40 and 128 Mel frequency bins), in order to assess their impact on command classification performance.
- **Lab Sync & Concept Review**: Met with Shuo to review semantic search engine progress and discuss audio processing concepts, including distinctions between spectrogram, Mel-spectrogram, and MFCCs, tool choice comparisons of Librosa vs TensorFlow for audio processing tasks, and data preprocessing techniques for audio signals. We also discussed next steps for literature reviews on emerging models as we begin to pivot towards fellowship project.
- **DREAM Fellowship Cohort Meeting**: Attended a meeting with my fellowship cohort featuring PIs who shared advice on exploring PhD opportunities, identifying and bridging skill gaps, building supportive research communities, and maintaining effective systems for organizing literature review and research data.

Thanks for following along. Looking forward to sharing what comes next!
